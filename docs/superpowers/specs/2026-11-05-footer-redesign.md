# Footer Redesign

## Intent

Bring the Home page footer into the LuxeStride cream and brown visual system and make its content easier to scan.

## Design

- Use a deep brown footer surface with cream text and warm brown or sand accents.
- Present three equal desktop columns: brand summary, useful page links, and social links.
- Use consistent spacing, readable link states, a subtle divider, and a current copyright line.
- Stack the columns on small screens.

## Approaches

- Selected: deep brown footer for strong separation from the light page and clear brand continuity.
- Considered: light cream footer, rejected because it provides less visual separation from the page content.
